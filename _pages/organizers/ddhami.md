**Devendra Singh Dhami** is an assistant professor at TU Eindhoven in the Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI) group. Previously, he was a DEPTH research group leader at hessian.AI and a post-doctoral researcher at TU Darmstadt under Kristian Kersting. His research interests are in Causality and Neuro-Symbolic AI, where his aim is to interconnect subfields to create systems that can not only learn but also successfully incorporate reasoning. He was the core organizer of the NeurIPS 2022 Workshop on neuro Causal and Symbolic AI, the AAAI 2023 and 2024 bridge on Continual Causality, and the AAAI workshop on "Are Large Language Models Simply Causal Parrots?". He is an electronic publishing editor of JAIR, an editorial board member for the Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence journal, and was a proceedings chair of SDM 2020 and serves as an area chair for major ML conferences such as NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR, as well as a topic editor and action editor in Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR). He has held lecture series on causality at ESSAI 2023 and 2024 and has given several talks at international workshops and outreach programs.
