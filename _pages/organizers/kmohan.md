**Karthika Mohan** is an Assistant Professor in the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Oregon State University, where she directs the Causal Intelligence and Reasoning Laboratory. Her research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and causal inference, with a focus on developing machine learning systems that are robust to distribution shifts, missing data, interference, and other challenges encountered in real-world deployment. She received her Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from UCLA under the supervision of Judea Pearl and subsequently held a postdoctoral position at UC Berkeley, where she was mentored by Stuart Russell. Her work has appeared in leading venues including NeurIPS, UAI, AISTATS, IJCAI, and JASA. Karthika has co-organized the UAI Workshop on Causal Inference, served as Sponsorship Co-Chair for UAI 2022, and is an Associate Editor of the Journal of Causal Inference. She regularly serves on program committees for major AI and machine learning conferences, including NeurIPS, ICML, UAI, AAAI, IJCAI, and AISTATS, and is actively involved in building the causal AI research community through service, mentoring, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
