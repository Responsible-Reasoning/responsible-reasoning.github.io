**Karthika Mohan** is an Assistant Professor in the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Oregon State University, where she directs the Causal Intelligence and Reasoning Laboratory. Her research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and causal inference, focusing on machine learning systems that are robust to distribution shifts, missing data, interference, and other real-world challenges. She received her PhD in AI from UCLA under Judea Pearl and was a postdoc at UC Berkeley with Stuart Russell. Her work has appeared at NeurIPS, UAI, AISTATS, IJCAI, and JASA. She co-organized the UAI Workshop on Causal Inference, served as Sponsorship Co-Chair for UAI 2022, and is an Associate Editor of the Journal of Causal Inference. She regularly serves on program committees for NeurIPS, ICML, UAI, AAAI, IJCAI, and AISTATS, and is actively involved in building the causal AI research community through service, mentoring, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
