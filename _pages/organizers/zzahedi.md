**Zahra Zahedi** is a Scientist at Honda Research Institute USA. Her research focuses on responsible reasoning in human-centered AI, studying how intelligent agents can reason under uncertainty while remaining aligned with human goals, expectations, and values. She develops probabilistic and computational approaches for reasoning, decision-making, and adaptation, combining Bayesian inference, causal inference, sequential decision-making, and multi-agent AI. She received her Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Arizona State University, where she studied computational accounts of trust in human-AI interaction. Her work has appeared in venues including AAAI, IJCAI, HRI, AAMAS, and RA-L, spanning human-AI collaboration, explainable AI, and adaptive intelligent systems. She is actively involved in the research community through conference organization, Area Chair service, and reviewing for leading AI, machine learning, and human-robot interaction venues.
