**Simon Kohaut** is a PhD candidate at the Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Lab at TU Darmstadt, advised by Prof. Kristian Kersting and co-supervised by Julian Eggert at Honda Research Institute EU. His research focuses on trustworthy autonomous systems, combining neuro-symbolic and reactive approaches for probabilistic modeling across perception, prediction, planning, and control. He has published in ITSC, IROS, IEEE Transactions on ITS, ACM JATS, and DMLR, and has reviewed for ITSC, IROS, CoRL, AAAI, AISTATS, NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR.
