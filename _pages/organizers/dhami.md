**Devendra Singh Dhami** is an Assistant Professor at TU Eindhoven in the Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence group. Previously he was a DEPTH research group leader at hessian.AI and a postdoctoral researcher at TU Darmstadt under Kristian Kersting. His research interests are in causality and neuro-symbolic AI, aiming to interconnect subfields to create systems that can not only learn but also successfully incorporate reasoning. He was a core organizer of the NeurIPS 2022 Workshop on Neuro Causal and Symbolic AI, the AAAI 2023 and 2024 bridge on Continual Causality, and the AAAI workshop "Are Large Language Models Simply Causal Parrots?". He is an electronic publishing editor of JAIR, an editorial board member for Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, was a proceedings chair of SDM 2020, and serves as an area chair for NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR as well as a topic editor and action editor for TMLR. He has held lecture series on causality at ESSAI 2023 and 2024, and given several talks at international workshops and outreach programs.
