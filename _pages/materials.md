After the workshop, this page will host its core material and outcomes for access beyond the conference days:

- **Digital posters** of all accepted papers, presented in an engaging form and maintained long after the workshop
- **Additional material** provided by the authors and speakers, such as video recordings or interactive web presences
- **A summarizing blog post** of the panel discussion, structuring and preserving its key arguments

While the workshop will be held as an in-person event at ICLR 2027, we are committed to making its outcomes available virtually. For authors unable to attend, e.g., due to visa issues or other exceptional personal circumstances, we will offer help to still show the poster, and otherwise host and promote a recorded video of the authors to make up for the gap in the poster session.
